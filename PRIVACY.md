## YOUR MAC. YOUR MODS. YOUR DATA.

**ADOFAI Mod Installer for macOS does not want your personal information.**

The installer contains no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, user accounts, profiling systems, or background data collection. It performs its work locally on your Mac.

### 🛡️ WHAT IT ACCESSES

The installer only accesses files required to configure ADOFAI modding:

- Your local ADOFAI installation
- The ADOFAI `Mods` folder
- Relevant Steam configuration files
- Automatic backups created before changes are made

### 🌐 NETWORK ACTIVITY

Internet access is used only when required to download publicly available loader or mod files from sources such as GitHub and the mod developers’ download pages.

Those external services may receive ordinary connection information—such as your IP address—under their own privacy policies.

### 🚫 WHAT WE DO NOT COLLECT

- Names or email addresses
- Steam login details
- Passwords
- Gameplay history
- Workshop activity
- Personal documents
- Advertising identifiers
- Usage analytics
- Mysterious “diagnostic” data

### ⚙️ LOCAL-FIRST OPERATION

No private information is uploaded to this project. No hidden service runs after installation. No account is created. No database is waiting in the shadows.

Uninstall it, delete it, inspect the source, or rebuild it yourself.

> **THE INSTALLER MODIFIES THE GAME. IT DOES NOT MONITOR THE PLAYER.**
